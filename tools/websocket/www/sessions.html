Every session whose end the observer reported while this page was open, newest first. A session that is still running is on the connections page; this one only grows, so it keeps the most recent few hundred and forgets the rest. Uptime is the session itself and setup is the dial and handshake before it. Height is what the peer claimed in its handshake, and is empty for a session that handshook before this page connected. A session with no message received taught us nothing: the peer completed the handshake, said nothing and went away, which a fleet of such peers does on a timer. Attempts that never reached a session are not here, and counting attempts by outcome belongs in a metrics endpoint rather than in a log.
