p2p-observer

Live view of what the observer's connections report, served by the websocket tool from the observer's NATS events. The tool keeps nothing: each page builds what it shows from the events that arrive while it is open, so it starts empty and fills as the network talks. For anything that happened before you opened it, replay an archive.

connections

Every open connection with what the peer told us in its handshake, its round trips and the kernel's view of the socket. Only the rows in view are rendered, so the table holds up at tens of thousands of connections.

blocks

How each block reached our peers: the share of connections that had announced it over the seconds after the first announcement, with the time axis following the slowest 99th percentile drawn.
