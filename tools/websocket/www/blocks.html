waiting for the first block announcement

The chart shows, per block, the share of its announcements that had arrived so many seconds after the first one; t50, t90 and t99 are that curve's 50%, 90% and 99% points. The time axis ends at the slowest t99 among the drawn curves, so it follows the data rather than a fixed window. A block without a height never connected to the observer's header tree: a block of another chain, or one announced by a peer far behind. Probes are compact block round trips, which the observer only sends when it is configured to.
